---
title: "Heat Stress and DCAD: Why Summer Rations Need a Second Look"
date: "2026-06-26"
---

Heat-stressed cows pant to dissipate heat, and panting drives respiratory alkalosis — blood pH rises as CO2 is blown off faster than normal. That shifts the physiological starting point a lactating ration's DCAD is working against, which is part of why the same lactating DCAD target that worked well in spring can under-perform in July and August.

Many nutritionists raise lactating DCAD moderately in summer to help offset that respiratory alkalosis and support rumen and systemic buffering during heat stress — the opposite direction of the close-up program, and a useful reminder that DCAD targets are period- and condition-specific, not a single number to set once a year.
