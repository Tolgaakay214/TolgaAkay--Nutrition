---
title: "NDFd at 30h vs. 240h — Which Number Should You Trust?"
date: "2026-07-24"
---

Neither in isolation — they answer different questions. NDFd at 30 hours approximates the digestibility relevant to a cow's actual rumen retention time and is generally the better predictor of intake and milk production response. NDFd at 240 hours approximates the theoretical maximum, "potential" digestibility of the fiber if it stayed in the rumen indefinitely — useful for characterizing fiber quality and comparing forage sources, less useful for predicting what a lactating cow will actually do with it.

When a lab report shows a forage with strong 240h NDFd but mediocre 30h NDFd, that's a fiber source that digests well eventually but slowly — a meaningfully different feeding proposition than one strong at both timepoints.
