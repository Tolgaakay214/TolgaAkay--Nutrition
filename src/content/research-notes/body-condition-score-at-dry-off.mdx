---
title: "Body Condition Score at Dry-Off: The Number That Predicts Transition Problems"
date: "2026-07-17"
---

Body condition score at dry-off is one of the more reliable single predictors of transition-period risk available on a commercial dairy, and it's free to collect. Cows drying off over-conditioned (BCS 4.0+ on a 5-point scale) mobilize more body fat in early lactation, increasing risk of hepatic lipidosis, ketosis, and reduced dry matter intake — a cascade that starts well before calving.

The target isn't a single number but a tight range across the dry group — roughly 3.0–3.25 at dry-off for most Holstein herds — because condition should change minimally during the dry period. Wide BCS variation within a dry-cow group is itself a signal that far-off feeding management, not just close-up formulation, needs attention.
