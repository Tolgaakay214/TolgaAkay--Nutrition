---
title: "Why \"High Protein\" Diets Aren't Always the Efficient Choice"
date: "2026-07-03"
---

Crude protein percentage is a blunt instrument. Two rations at the same CP% can differ substantially in how much of that nitrogen is actually captured as milk protein versus excreted as urea — driven by amino acid balance, rumen-degradable versus undegradable protein split, and how well energy and protein availability are synchronized in the rumen.

Pushing CP% up to chase milk protein yield, without addressing that balance, tends to raise milk urea nitrogen and feed cost more than it raises milk protein — an inefficient trade both nutritionally and economically, and one that shows up as a real, avoidable cost on a feed bill.
