---
title: "Water Intake and Dry Matter Intake: An Underrated Relationship"
date: "2026-07-10"
---

Water intake and dry matter intake move together closely enough that a water problem is frequently misdiagnosed as a feed problem. A lactating cow producing 40+ kg of milk needs on the order of 4–5 kg of water per kg of DM consumed — restrict water access, availability, or quality, and DMI drops within days, often before anyone traces the cause back to the water source.

Trough space, flow rate, and cleanliness are worth auditing with the same rigor as bunk space when intake unexpectedly declines — particularly after pen moves or during hot weather, when water demand rises sharply and undersized or poorly placed troughs become the actual limiting factor.
