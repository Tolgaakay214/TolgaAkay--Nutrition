---
title: "Does Particle Size Always Improve Rumination?"
date: "2026-08-07"
---

Not linearly, and not indefinitely. Longer particle size generally stimulates more chewing and rumination up to a point — but past that point, excessively long or coarse forage particles increase sorting risk and can actually reduce effective fiber intake if enough of the long fraction ends up left in the bunk rather than consumed.

The relationship is closer to a curve than a straight line: rumination responds to physically effective fiber that is actually consumed, not to top-screen percentage in the mixer wagon. A ration with impressive top-screen numbers but heavy refusal sorting can under-deliver on rumination compared to a slightly shorter-chopped, better-consumed ration.
